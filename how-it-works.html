A FEW PRACTICAL NOTES

Make a good
connection.

What should I ask before collecting?＋

Confirm dimensions, quantity, condition, access, loading help, pickup timing, and the agreed price. Photos help clarify details in advance.

Can I list imperfect or part-used materials?＋

Yes, when they are safe and useful for another project. Describe wear, part-used quantities, and any known limitations accurately.

Does ReBuild manage payments or transport?＋

This frontend prototype does not handle payments or transport. Buyers and sellers arrange both directly. A production service would need clear policies and secure workflows.
