GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
Better building.

New to reusing surplus? Start here, or ask our ReBuild helper.

What can I list on ReBuild Exchange?＋

Usable surplus from construction, renovation, or fit-out projects: timber, tiles, paint, fixtures, bricks, tools, and more. Describe the quantity and condition clearly.

How do pickup and payment work?＋

Buyer and seller coordinate directly. Agree the price, payment method, and a safe pickup time before travelling. ReBuild Exchange is a discovery platform in this frontend demo.

Are materials inspected or guaranteed?＋

Listings are provided by community members. Ask the seller for details and inspect materials before agreeing to a reuse. This demo does not verify sellers or listings.

How are sustainability estimates calculated?＋

Impact numbers are illustrative estimates based on listed quantities and typical material weights. They are not measured carbon savings or a product lifecycle assessment.
