HOW WE THINK ABOUT IMPACT

Transparent estimates.
Honest expectations.

Weight estimates can be calculated by multiplying listed quantities by a typical unit weight for the material. A fuller future model could include material type, quality, reuse outcome, and region-specific disposal data. None of these figures alone proves avoided emissions.
